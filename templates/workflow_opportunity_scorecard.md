## Stop screen

Course 1 does not continue if any answer is **yes**.

- [ ] Real employer, customer, employee, supplier, patient, or other personal
      data would be needed.
- [ ] The workflow could decide diagnosis, treatment, employment, credit,
      insurance, education, benefits, law enforcement, migration, or another
      consequential matter about a person.
- [ ] The first version would send, pay, purchase, delete, grant access, sign,
      or change a source system automatically.
- [ ] No process owner can define the rules and exceptions.
- [ ] No authorised reviewer or manual fallback exists.
- [ ] The proposed benefit requires hiding material limitations.

If checked: stop, record the issue, and select a lower-risk synthetic process or
obtain the appropriate professional and organisational review.
