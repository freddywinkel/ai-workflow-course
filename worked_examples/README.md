# Course 1 Completed Worksheet Examples

These are completed fictional examples, not blank forms and not learner
answers. A module tells you when to read one. First follow the example, then
copy the corresponding blank template and recreate the method for the
different Synthetic Small and Medium-sized Enterprise (SME) Operations
Exception Assistant.

All names, roles, records, results, and decisions are synthetic. None of these
examples authorizes real data, a client trial, production use, or an external
action.

Module 9 includes a completed assessment record as well as the UAT, adoption,
and handover examples. The assessment example demonstrates the required
six-area calculation and ten oral answers; it is not permission to copy its
scores or answers into your different capstone.
